How it loads more videos

The overlay runs on top of the live X.com feed. When you reach the last 2 queued videos, the extension programmatically scrolls the hidden X.com page behind the overlay — X loads the next batch, the MutationObserver picks them up, and they're silently added to the queue. Seamless.
